RAAMP ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application and related services (the "Service").

Section 01

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

a) Account Information

  • Name and email address you provide during signup
  • Password (stored as a secure hash — never in plain text)

b) Connected Platform Data — Meta / Instagram / Facebook (optional)

If you connect your Instagram Business account or Facebook Page to RAAMP, we may store:

  • Account/Page identifiers (e.g., Page ID, IG Business ID)
  • Access tokens (stored securely and never exposed publicly)
  • Comment/post identifiers and webhook event metadata needed to deliver enabled features (e.g., auto-replies) and maintain auditability

Optional Integration

Connecting your Meta / Instagram / Facebook account is entirely optional. You can disconnect at any time from within your account settings, and associated tokens will be deleted immediately.

c) Usage and Technical Data

  • Basic activity logs (feature usage, timestamps) and error diagnostics
  • Device/browser information and IP address (for security and troubleshooting)
Section 02

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate RAAMP's services
  • Enable integrations you choose to connect — for example, to receive comment events via webhooks and post auto-replies when that feature is enabled
  • Monitor performance, troubleshoot issues, and prevent misuse
  • Maintain security and integrity of the Service
We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for third-party purposes, or sale to data brokers.
Section 03

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Our Commitment

We do not sell your personal data. Period.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers that help us operate the Service (see Section 6 for specific providers)
  • If required by law, court order, or binding legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the Service and its users
Section 04

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These measures include, but are not limited to: encrypted storage of sensitive tokens, hashed passwords, and access-controlled infrastructure.

Security Limitation

No system is completely secure. While we take every reasonable precaution, we cannot guarantee absolute security against all possible threats.
Section 05

5. Data Retention (Specific Periods)

Where possible, RAAMP uses specific, defined retention periods:

Data Type Retention Period Trigger for Deletion
Account data Active account lifetime + 30 days Verified deletion request
Integration tokens While integration is connected Disconnect or deletion request
Webhook / audit records (e.g., auto-reply logs) Up to 90 days Automatic expiry
Operational logs Up to 30 days Automatic expiry
Some limited information may be retained beyond these periods where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
